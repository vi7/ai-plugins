# Allegro delivery and Smart: only as much as the decision needs

Official Allegro help (checked 22 October 2026) describes **two transitional Smart threshold schemes**:
- New or renewed Smart subscriptions from 2 March 2026: **49.90 PLN per seller**, for eligible parcel lockers, pickup points or courier delivery.
- Older subscriptions still in their previous period: **45 PLN per seller** for pickup/parcel locker, **65 PLN per seller** for courier.

These are conditional rules, not proof of a user's active membership, method eligibility or free checkout delivery. Do **not** repeatedly ask about Smart for a cheap purchase: treat it as unknown unless the user states their status. Check current official rules when the outcome depends on the threshold. Also confirm the offer supports eligible Smart delivery; exclusions and special shipments can apply.

**Basket arithmetic:** multiply offer price by necessary packs; sum once per product. Group shipping by *confirmed* seller, never across unrelated sellers. A public offer's "delivery from X PLN" is a method-dependent starting price, not a verified checkout amount. Display the verified merchandise subtotal separately if an exact full delivered price cannot be established. Do not add per-item delivery charges repeatedly when the same seller may consolidate, and do not assume consolidation is guaranteed. ETA is an estimate, not a shipping guarantee. Ignore coupon/Smart savings that are not confirmed for this account.

Check seller/product reputation separately only if relevant to the choice. Product stars do not transfer to an unverified seller.
